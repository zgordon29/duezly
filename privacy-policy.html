Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Duezly ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our SMS homework reminder service.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following information to provide our service:

  • Mobile phone number — used solely to deliver SMS reminders to you.
  • Canvas Calendar link — a read-only calendar feed link used to retrieve your assignment data from Canvas. We do not access your Canvas account directly and do not store your Canvas credentials.
  • Course and assignment data — fetched from your Canvas calendar feed to generate your daily reminders. This data is not stored permanently.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used exclusively to:

  • Send you daily SMS reminders about upcoming homework and assignments.
  • Retrieve assignment data from your Canvas calendar feed to generate your reminders.
We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your mobile phone number or personal information with any third party for marketing or commercial purposes.

3. SMS Messaging Disclosures

By checking the SMS consent checkbox on the Duezly sign-in page and providing your mobile phone number during onboarding, you consent to receive recurring automated SMS text messages from Duezly sent from +1 (678) 578-2152. Your consent to receive SMS messages is not a condition of purchase.

  • Message frequency: approximately 1 message per day while you are enrolled.
  • Message and data rates may apply. Check with your mobile carrier for details.
  • To stop receiving messages at any time, reply STOP to any message or contact us directly.
  • To get help, reply HELP or contact us at the address below.
We will never sell, rent, trade, or share your mobile phone number or SMS opt-in data with any third party for marketing or promotional purposes.

4. Data Storage and Security

We store your phone number, email address, and Canvas Calendar link using industry-standard cloud security (AWS DynamoDB). Your phone number is used only to deliver SMS reminders and is never shared with third parties for any other purpose. Your Canvas Calendar link is used only to fetch your assignment data and is never shared. We retain your data only as long as you are an active user of the service.

5. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to operate Duezly:

  • Twilio — SMS delivery. Your phone number is transmitted to Twilio solely to send messages. Twilio's privacy policy applies to their handling of this data.
  • Stripe — payment processing. Your payment information is collected and stored directly by Stripe. We do not store your credit card number or payment details. Stripe's privacy policy applies to their handling of payment data.
  • Google — account authentication via Google Sign-In. We receive your name and email address from Google solely to identify your account. Google's privacy policy applies to their handling of authentication data.
  • Canvas LMS (Instructure) — your institution's learning management system. We access only your public Canvas Calendar link to retrieve assignment data necessary to generate your reminders.
  • Amazon Web Services (AWS) — cloud infrastructure for running the service.

6. Your Rights

You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us. Upon request, we will delete your phone number and Canvas Calendar link from our systems within 7 days.

7. Children's Privacy

Duezly is intended for use by college students aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the service after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, contact us at: hello@duezly.com
