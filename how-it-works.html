How Duezly Works

Opt-in flow and SMS consent screen — publicly available for reviewer verification.

📋 For Twilio / Carrier Reviewers

The SMS opt-in for Duezly occurs after users sign in with Google and complete payment. Because this step is behind authentication, this page provides a public preview of the exact consent screen, including the full checkbox language, so you can verify the opt-in without going through checkout.

User Sign-Up Flow
1

Visit duezly.com and sign in with Google

Users arrive at duezly.com and click "Get started." They authenticate with their Google account — only their email is used to create an account.

2

Complete $1.99/month Stripe checkout

Users are directed to a Stripe-hosted payment page to subscribe. No trial — subscription begins immediately.

3

Onboarding: enter phone number and give SMS consent

After payment, users land on the onboarding page (shown below). They enter their phone number, Canvas Calendar link, and preferred send time, then check the mandatory SMS consent checkbox before submitting. The checkbox is unchecked by default and submission is blocked without it.

4

Daily SMS reminders begin

Duezly sends one SMS per day at the user's chosen time listing assignments due today, tomorrow, and later that week — pulled from their Canvas calendar.
