Second, the "Duezly Settings" section of the Duezly dashboard (duezly.com/dashboard.html) always displays the same optional, unchecked consent checkbox above the Save changes button, allowing subscribers to opt in or out of SMS reminders at any time. By checking this box and saving, you expressly consent to receive recurring automated SMS text messages from Duezly sent from +1 (678) 578-2152.
