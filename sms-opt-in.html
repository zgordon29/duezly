SMS Opt-In

This page describes how Duezly collects SMS consent and the exact opt-in language shown to users during enrollment.

Program Overview

Service name

Duezly — automated daily SMS homework reminders for Canvas LMS users.

Sender number

+1 (678) 578-2152 — all messages are sent from this number.

Message type

Recurring automated SMS notifications — approximately 1 message per day while enrolled, containing a summary of assignments and due dates pulled from the user's Canvas calendar feed.

STOP / HELP keywords

  • STOP — immediately unsubscribes the number; one confirmation message is sent and no further messages follow.
  • HELP — replies with support contact info (hello@duezly.com).

Message & data rates

Standard message and data rates from the user's mobile carrier may apply.

How Opt-In Is Collected

Duezly collects SMS consent at two points in the user flow, both before any messages are ever sent. Consent is never required to complete a purchase — the service can be subscribed to and managed without opting in to SMS.

Step 1 — Subscribe page (duezly.com/purchase.html)

After signing in with Google, the user lands on the Subscribe page before entering payment. This page includes an optional phone number field and an optional, unchecked SMS consent checkbox with full disclosure language. The "Subscribe — $1.99/mo" button works regardless of whether these fields are filled in.

Screenshot of duezly.com/purchase.html — the consent checkbox is optional and unchecked by default. The Subscribe button works whether or not it is checked.

Step 2 — Dashboard setup card (duezly.com/dashboard.html)

After subscribing, users who have not yet provided a phone number, Canvas Calendar link, or SMS consent are shown a prominent "Set Up Duezly" card at the top of their dashboard. This card contains all required reminder settings plus the same optional, unchecked SMS consent checkbox. Daily reminders are only sent once the phone number, Canvas link, and SMS consent are all saved.

Screenshot of duezly.com/dashboard.html for a subscribed user who hasn't completed setup. The SMS consent checkbox is optional and unchecked by default.

Consent to receive SMS messages is not a condition of purchase or use of any Duezly service. Users may subscribe and manage their account without ever opting in to SMS.
