🔗 How to find your Canvas iCal URL

  1. Go to your school's Canvas site and log in.
  2. Click Calendar in the left sidebar.
  3. Scroll to the bottom right and click Calendar Feed.
  4. Copy the URL — it will contain instructure.com/feeds/calendars/
  5. Paste it in your dashboard after signing in.
