Before you start

Find your Canvas calendar link

  1. Log in to your school's Canvas site.
  2. Click Calendar in the left sidebar.
  3. At the bottom right, click Calendar Feed.
  4. Copy the link. It contains instructure.com/feeds/calendars/
  5. Paste it when you set up Duezly.
